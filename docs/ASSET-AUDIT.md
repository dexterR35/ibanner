# Assets and fixtures · 28 October 2026

The project ships **no example banners, brand logos, brand photography or brand copy**. The earlier bundled example artwork (`assets/`, `public/references/`, the reference contact sheet and decoded frames) and its manifest were removed on 28 October 2026 at the user's request. Git history still holds them; nothing in the app loads them.

## What ships

- **Blueprints only.** Starter layouts for BANNER1 and BANNER2 (eight sizes each) and Banner 3 (11 sizes: nine GIFs, two static). They are layout instructions (boxes, fades, GIF parts and timing), not pixels. JSON snapshots are under `blueprints/`.
- **Blank campaigns.** New campaigns use placeholder copy (`YOUR HEADLINE / GOES HERE`, `YOUR OFFER / TEXT`, `Call to action`), an empty legal line, no photo and a neutral `LOGO` text placeholder until a logo is uploaded.
- **No legal copy is invented.** Every market starts with an empty legal field and a quality warning until reviewed copy is entered.

## Uploaded originals

Uploaded photos, logos and fonts are stored in IndexedDB under their SHA-256 hash and are never modified. Workspace backups include them and verify the hashes on import.

## Test fixtures

`tests/fixtures/` holds neutral synthetic images generated by `npm run fixtures:make` (`scripts/make-fixtures.py`, needs Pillow):

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `photo-300x600.png`, `photo-160x600.png`, `photo-320x480.png` | Gradient scene with one round "subject"; upload, crop and subject-placement tests |
| `text-banner-300x600.png` | Plain text banner (LOGO / BIG HEADLINE / SPECIAL OFFER / Sign up / legal line); Florence smoke check |
| `ocr-banner-300x600.png` | Flat colour blocks at measured OCR line boxes; colour sampling in the OCR import test |

They contain no brand names or artwork.

## Fidelity

Starting layouts are editable drafts, not approved golden fixtures. The red default accent and glow colour are starting values, not brand tokens. There is no rising-arrow or other offer graphic.
